❓ Dashboard Help

What each section does, with an example of when to use it.

📊 Dashboard
Overview of your store: sales, orders, and quick stats for today.
Example: Open this each morning to see how many orders came in overnight and your running sales total.
📝 Orders
See incoming and past orders from your POS.
Example: A customer disputes a receipt — find their order here by date and check the items and total.
📦 Inventory
Track stock levels for your raw materials and supplies, and add new items.
Example: You restock 50 sacks of rice — find "Rice 25kg" here and use +Add to increase its stock count.
📂 Categories
Organize your products and inventory items into categories.
Example: Group all your drinks together — create a "Drinks" category, then assign it to Coke, Sprite, and Water.
✏️ Products
Add, edit, or remove the items you sell — set price, photo, and category.
Example: You start selling a new flavor — click +Add Item, set its name, price, and photo; it appears instantly on the cashier.
👥 Staff
Add your staff here — create their accounts, set name, password, and control access.
Example: Hiring a new cashier — add their name and a password here; they use it to log in on the POS.
📋 Activity Log
See a log of staff actions and submitted receipts — view, accept, or mark as invalid.
Example: A cashier applied a big discount — check Activity Log to see who did it and when, then approve or flag it.
💬 Messages
Send and receive messages with your staff.
Example: Tell your staff about today's promo — type a message here and it shows up on their cashier screen.
📊 Sales Report
View your sales totals, best sellers, and reports by day or date range.
Example: Checking which item sold best last week — open Sales Report, pick the date range, and see your top sellers.
✅ Daily Checklist
Set tasks your staff must complete when opening or closing the store.
Example: Make sure staff count the cash drawer every closing — add "Count cash drawer" as a checklist task.
💰 Other Expenses
Record other costs like rent, bills, or supplies that are not part of your inventory.
Example: You paid for electricity this month — log it here so it's included in your real profit calculation.
⚙️ Settings
Update your store details, currency, tax rate, and account settings.
Example: Your area's tax rate changed — update the Tax Rate field here so receipts calculate it correctly.
🎨 Customize POS
Customize how your POS screen looks — colors, logo, and layout.
Example: Want your POS to match your shop colors — pick your brand color as Accent and upload your logo.
🔗 Cashier Link
Get the store code or link your staff use to connect their POS device.
Example: Setting up a new POS terminal — copy the Store Code here and paste it into the cashier app on first launch.
💻 My POS
Open your store POS screen to start taking orders.
Example: Ready to start selling — click My POS to open the live cashier screen yourself.
